### 4 — #24: `_fail_step` is not lease-fenced

`packages/jobs/python/papertree_jobs/store.py:353` writes `state='failed'` with
`WHERE job_id = ? AND step_name = ?` and no `lease_owner` predicate. The **success** path is
fenced — `runner.py` calls `check_lease()` immediately before `_finish_step`, with a comment
explaining exactly why. The failure path (`runner.py:88`) has no such guard.

A superseded worker whose step body throws can overwrite the live worker's committed
`succeeded` checkpoint with `failed`, after which a step whose side effect already committed
runs again — precisely what the `recorded.state == "succeeded"` early-return exists to prevent.

**Write the failing test first**, in `packages/jobs/python/tests/test_durability.py`: worker A
loses its lease mid-step, worker B reclaims and commits `succeeded`, then A's body raises. Assert
B's checkpoint survives and the step does not re-run. Confirm it fails, then fix, then confirm it
passes.

Keep the existing 9 durability + 11 jobs-api tests green.
