# PaperTree metrics

This is the release-history metrics page. Every release adds one column to the table in "The releases at a glance" and one "vX against vY" section, so that anyone can see what a release measured and how it got better (or worse) than the one before. Rules: every number is re-measured for that release and names its raw source, never carried over from an older page; a comparison is made only between like and like (the same papers, the same judge panel, the same kind of run); what got worse is written down with its cause; a result that is only partly there is written as PARTIAL and is not rounded up. The v0.1 column was completed after the final gate and the final hands-on walk (S9) on the release head `60ae507`; the walk's full feature table is `research/build/READER-RELEASE-RESULT.md`. Evidence is cited by label (for example `PaperTree-evidence/01-discovery/journey-baseline.md`); the evidence store is kept outside the repository.

v0.1 is the first release, so it is compared against two pre-release baselines: the discovery walk at `6ff15ad` (2026-09-25) and the handoff gate at `45fb2db` (2026-09-26). The journeys are the ones in ADR-002: A upload and read, B highlights, C grounded explanation and summary, D canvas, E evidence of which code and provider ran, F the gates and CI.

v0.2.0 is compared against v0.1.0. Its column was completed after S8 (#320), from CI run 39015930115 (the head of #320, `cbe70fa`, merged into `release/v0.2`), the local runs on `91857a4` and `065f709`, and `research/build/v0.2-ui/RESULT.md`. v0.2 had no hands-on walk on the live model and no live-journey CI run. Its evidence is the e2e suite in CI against a faux agent (88 specs, among them v0.1's journey B, C and D specs and S8's 14-step flow), S8's numbers at scale, and five live calls (RESULT.md, "Live AI spend"). So the journey rows say what v0.2 ran, not a hands-on verdict. Where v0.2 changed nothing, the cell says so and is not re-measured: no v0.1 number is repeated as if v0.2 had measured it. The rows below the v0.1 ones are new in v0.2.

### Accepted regressions

1. **ResNet pooled reading order 0.932 to 0.906** (per-page 0.929 to 0.894). Cause: the paragraph-split rule (`b96ed98`) splits runs of printed paragraphs that the gold boxes as one region, so those regions stop matching and their pairs leave the pool (scored pairs 103 at base, 85 at head; 116 to 80 across the rule's own commit). All 8 discordant pairs left at head are against annotation defects in the gold (footnotes the guide says are out of body order; a second wide figure box drawn after the text). The parser's own real discordant pairs on ResNet went from 2 to 0. Accepted by the S2R review; the gold files are not this track's to edit, and the two defects go to the gold review (#54). The owner still has to ratify the merge-rule exception.
2. **GPT-3 macro-F1 0.3429 to 0.3416 (-0.0013)**; paragraph F1 0.190 to 0.174. Cause: two extra predicted paragraph blocks (18 to 20; page 30 correctly rises from 1 to 7, page 15 falls from 15 to 11) against a gold that holds only 3 paragraph regions on those pages. A precision effect of a correct split on a three-region gold. The S2 report's own explanation was wrong; the S2R review replaced it.

Other per-type F1 drops over 0.05, all from the same paragraph-split rule against multi-paragraph gold boxes: a3c paragraph 0.417 to 0.357, bert paragraph 0.545 to 0.342, resnet paragraph 0.575 to 0.404; and attention caption 0.667 to 0.500 (the raw gold types that caption box as a figure).

Tuning risk: all thresholds were chosen on the same 14 papers they are scored on, and there is no held-out paper; the paragraph cues sit on a plateau (indent 0.3 to 0.8 em and skip 0.25 to 0.4 em give identical results), which argues against fitting to the gold but is not an out-of-sample test.
